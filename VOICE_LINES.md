# Voice lines

Use WAV files in:

`MelonLoader/UserData/HomelanderNPC/Audio/`

Files:

- intro.wav
- taunt.wav
- attack.wav
- hurt.wav
- death.wav
- heatvision.wav

The included loader accepts uncompressed PCM WAV files at 8-bit or 16-bit depth.

For a Homelander-inspired test character, record or generate original lines rather than extracting dialogue from the show.
